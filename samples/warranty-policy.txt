Nordwind Store — Warranty Policy

All products come with a limited warranty against manufacturing defects. The
warranty period depends on the product category: outerwear and footwear are
covered for two years, apparel and fleece for one year, and Basecamp backpacks
carry a lifetime warranty. Tents are covered for three years.

The warranty covers seam failures, broken zippers, delamination and hardware
defects that appear during normal use. It does not cover normal wear and tear,
damage caused by misuse, accidents, improper care (for example machine-washing
nubuck boots), alterations, or damage from prolonged UV exposure.

To make a warranty claim, email warranty@nordwind.example with your order
number, photos of the defect and a short description. We reply within two
business days. If the claim is approved we will repair the item, replace it,
or issue store credit — at our discretion. Shipping the item to us for
inspection is free within the EU; we cover return shipping in all cases.

The warranty is valid only for purchases made in the official Nordwind store
or from authorized resellers, and it is non-transferable.
