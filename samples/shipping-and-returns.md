# Nordwind Store — Shipping & Returns

## Shipping

We ship worldwide from our warehouse in Vilnius.

| Method | Time | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5–7 business days | $4.99 |
| Express | 1–2 business days | $14.99 |
| Same-day (Vilnius only) | before 22:00 | $9.99 |

Orders over **$50** ship free with standard delivery. Orders placed before
14:00 CET are dispatched the same day.

We currently **do not ship** to the following countries: Cuba, Iran, North Korea,
Syria.

## Order tracking

After your order ships you will receive a tracking link by email. If the
tracking page shows no updates for 48 hours, contact support@nordwind.example.

## Returns

- You can return any item within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.
- Items must be unused and in original packaging.
- Personalized items and gift cards are **non-refundable**.
- To start a return, email support@nordwind.example with your order number.
- Refunds are processed within 5 business days after we receive the item.
- Return shipping is free for defective items; otherwise it costs $3.99,
  deducted from the refund.

## Payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal and Apple Pay. We do not accept cash on
delivery or cryptocurrency.
